DropSort brings the world of Fluorescent Activated Sorting to microfluidic droplet devices. All you need is a microscope and a microfluidic sorter chip, and we provide all the comfort you are used from cell based sorting.

Flexible Gating

DropSort features arbitrary shape four-point gating and threshold-based gating in histogram views. Gates can be nested hierarchically with up to three sub-gates. Each gate is fully configurable with all channels.

For Every Need

We support up to three fluorescent channels and droplet length to be gated on. Each channel has a sampling rate of 100 kHz and an input range of 0-1V.

Easy Integration

Your data is exported into the Flow Cytometry Standard (.fcs) file format for easy integration into your downstream processing pipeline. Annotation templates let you save comments and acquisition parameters of your setup with each experiment.

How does it work?

We will provide you with an acquisition card and our DropSort 2020 software (Windows 10, MacOS, Linux. Ethernet port required). All you need to do is to plug your PMTs into the acquisition card and wire up your microfluidic chip*. We will support you with the setup.

*Depending on your setup this might require an external amplifier and function generator
